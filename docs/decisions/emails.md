Part of the decision record. [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) has the rules that apply to every change and the list of topics. Record decisions about this topic here, in the same change as the code.

Melvin, 2026-11-26: "go take a look at how github.com/mjparinas/liftsmith sends out daily emails for as many days as possible without repeating and without having boring or thin content. after a user registers and each email gives them information about an important/useful/fun feature of the app. they need to be branded with our branding and colour scheme. they should also be concise but engaging content. finally, each email should have a graphic or screenshot of the app. we should focus on features that all users have access to but a good portion of them should be paid features (properly emphasized that only the paid plan has it) … even things like did you know, and highlighting a feature. plus content that talks about how the app was carefully crafted for usability, optimized for speed and performance." The same message asked for a colour and branding sweep of every email Tuned already sends.

**Built** (2026-11-26). Modelled on Liftsmith's Getting started emails (`src/server/tour.ts`, `reminderEmails.ts`): a branded layout, a kicker with the day, a title, a few lines, a button and the real screen underneath. Unlike Liftsmith there is no separate weekly series: Tuned's whole series is daily, because it is about a month long.

## What Tuned sends

- **Getting to know Tuned** (`devices/lib/email-series.mjs`): a welcome, then **37 emails, one a day**, each about one thing. 26 are about what every copy does, 11 about Premium (pause, rewind and record; following teams; profiles; the Tuned account; the website; themes; several playlists; Trakt and Simkl; and a last email with everything Premium adds), 2 of them "Free + Premium" (lists, downloads). Among them: 3 "Did you know?" round-ups (remote and gesture tricks, TV settings, guide fixes) and 7 "Built with care" emails on speed, one stream at a time, waiting that's never a mystery, the player getting out of the way, privacy, recovering from dropped channels and reporting a problem. Each has a kicker ("Day 3 of 37 · Live TV"), a short title (the subject), one or two opening lines, three to six points, sometimes a "Did you know?" line, a button to a page on tunedplayer.com, and a picture.
- **The admin's alerts and morning digest** (`lib/admin/automations.mjs`, to `ADMIN_EMAILS` only): the same layout since 2026-11-26, with a button to the admin tab each is about, and the digest as a table.
- Nothing else: Stripe's receipts are Stripe's (its own branding settings), and Play's are Google's.

## How the series works

- **Who:** every Tuned account made on or after 2026-11-27 (`SERIES_START`), Google or Apple. Accounts from before then get nothing, because the series is for people who just signed up. There's no age on a Tuned account, so there's no under-18 rule.
- **When:** the hourly job `/api/cron/emails` (`vercel.json`, at :20, `CRON_SECRET`). The welcome goes out within the hour after sign-up; each next email waits at least 23 hours after the last, so it lands at about the hour the person signed up, one a day and never two. An account the job first sees more than two days after sign-up (sending wasn't set up yet) skips the welcome and starts at day 1. Accounts have no time zone, so the sign-up hour stands in for a good time to read.
- **The Premium line:** a Premium email (and a Free + Premium one) has a ★ Premium pill under the kicker and a boxed line fitted to the reader, from what the server already knows: a free account reads "Only in Tuned Premium: US$9.99 a year or US$24.99 once, for up to 3 devices. No ads either way." and the button becomes Get Tuned Premium (the account page, which sells it through Stripe); an account with Premium reads "You have Premium, so this is already yours"; an account with a device on the copy that has everything unlocked reads "Your copy of Tuned already has this unlocked". The pictures carry the same Premium pill.
- **Words:** each point is something the app does today (checked against `docs/decisions/` when written). Never a provider, channel or sports brand (the pictures use made-up teams and channels), never "GitHub" (website.md), never a sleep timer or a second stream on screen (scope.md), Canadian English. English only for now: accounts don't record a language, and the website is English. The emails test checks the word rules, ids, point counts and that every picture exists.
- **Every email** says Tuned is a player for your own playlists and provides no channels or content, why it was sent and which day it is, a one-click unsubscribe link (an HMAC of the account id with `EMAIL_SECRET`, so nothing is stored), the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers (RFC 8058, which Gmail and Yahoo require of bulk senders), and Tuned's postal address (`MAIL_POSTAL_ADDRESS`; CAN-SPAM and CASL). The unsubscribe link opens a page with one button that POSTs, so mail scanners that open links can't unsubscribe anyone; a mail app's own Unsubscribe POSTs straight to the same address.
- **On by default, one switch to stop** (as Liftsmith, where Melvin chose "on by default for all users" on 2026-11-14): the account page's sign-in card says new accounts get one short email a day for a few weeks, and **Emails, sign out or delete › Daily tips by email** turns them off or back on (`/api/account/email`). Unsubscribing from an email turns the same switch off. Deleting the account deletes both tables' rows.
- **What's kept** (`lib/email-store.mjs`, `email_prefs` and `email_sends` in `schema.sql`, also made on first use): whether the account wants the emails, and which keys went out when. Nothing about what anyone watches. The privacy policy says so.
- **Sending:** Resend's batch API, up to 100 emails a request with an idempotency key, so a rerun never sends twice and the shared Resend rate limit isn't hit one email at a time. The job does nothing until `RESEND_API_KEY`, `MAIL_FROM` (a sender on a domain verified in Resend, such as `Tuned <hello@tunedplayer.com>`), `MAIL_POSTAL_ADDRESS` and `EMAIL_SECRET` are all set; the admin's System tab lists them.

## The look

`tunedMail` in `lib/emails.mjs` is the one layout: the ink background (#121018) with the channel stack and "Tuned" at the top, the content on a card (#1e1c25, 20 px corners like the app's cards), a kicker in teal for free and craft emails, violet for Premium and gold for Did you know?, the ★ Premium pill in the violet gradient, points marked with a violet ▸, the Premium line boxed with a violet edge, Did you know? boxed with its label in teal, a fully round button in the app's dark-theme primary (#c9beff on #2b10a0), and the picture under the card. Plain text goes with every HTML email. Tables and inline styles only, so it holds up in Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail, and it's dark in every client (`color-scheme: dark`).

**Pictures** are `devices/public/email/<id>.jpg` (1200 × 760, shown 560 wide), drawn by `node devices/email-images.mjs <screens>` from the app's JVM screenshots (the Screenshots workflow's `screenshots/staging` branch) plus the website's Playlists and settings page: phones, TVs, a tablet and a browser in frames on Tuned's violet and teal glow, with the area's name and the Premium pill in the corner. The player's screens are its controls over black, so they're blended over a made-up colourful "picture". Where no screen shows the feature well (live events, following, one stream, speed, Trakt and Simkl, media servers, everything in Premium) it's a drawn graphic in the same style. `node devices/email-preview.mjs <folder> [free|premium|unlocked]` writes every email as HTML to look at.

## Keep it current

When a feature a person would notice ships, add an email for it to `SERIES` (or a point to an existing one), a picture in `email-images.mjs`, and a line here. Removing a feature removes or rewrites its email in the same pull request, like the rest of the decision record.
