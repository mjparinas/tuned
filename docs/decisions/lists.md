# Favourites and your own lists

**Lists like a music app's playlists** (Melvin, 2026-11-19: "groups should really be similar to how spotify does it. you have favourite a channel and that adds it to a favourite playlist, but then you can make your own playlists. so favourite is basically a playlist that you can add to by default but you can make your own"). They're called lists in the app, because "playlist" already means the provider's playlist (Settings › Playlists).

- **Favourites is the built-in list.** The heart adds to it in one tap, as before, and it keeps its own table (`favourites`), order (Settings › Order favourites), widget and Android TV row. Nothing about it moved, so no favourite was lost. It stays free for everyone (Melvin, 2026-11-19: "favourites should be unlocked for everyone though").
- **Whole shows are favourites too** (Melvin, 2026-11-27: "I can't seem to add actual full TV shows to the favorites list … I'm not talking about just the individual episodes. I'm talking about the actual show itself"). The heart on a show's page (the way in on a TV), and Add to favourites in a show poster's long-press menu on a phone, favourite the show itself; its poster then wears the heart and it sits in the Shows tab's Favourites row and Favourites chip, alongside any show with a favourite episode. A show has no row of its own, only its episodes, so its favourite is kept in the same `favourites` table under the show's own id, `StableId.show(showKey)` (FNV-1a 64 of `show|` and the show's normalized name, `data/FavouriteShows`). That needs no new table or migration, and the backup, the website's edits and the merge between devices carry it like any other favourite: the website's channels page never lists it but keeps it whole when a channel's heart is toggled. The Shows tab turns those ids back into show keys only while its Favourites chip is chosen, so browsing never waits on it. The TV apps keep their own favourites on the TV for now; when they sync with the account, a show's favourite is that same id as a whole-number string.
- **Watch later is part of Favourites** (Melvin, 2026-11-27, choosing "Merge into Favourites" after asking why the two overlapped): one heart saves a channel, a film or a whole show, and what was in Watch later moved in on its own ([playlist](playlist.md)).
- **Your own lists** sit beside it (`lists` and `list_items`, Room v18). "Add to list" on a channel's page or a film's page (and in their long-press menus) ticks the lists it belongs in, or makes a new one with it already inside. Each list is a chip straight after Favourites on Live TV and Movies, shown on a tab only once it holds something of that kind. Browse chips are filters only: they do not show **Rename list** or **Delete list** beside them (Melvin, 2026-11-22: "Remove rename list and delete list on the chips on live tv", then the same for Movies; Shows have no list chips). On the app, rename and delete live in Settings › Channels and lists › Manage lists ([settings](settings.md)); on Android TV, rename stays on the phone or website and Manage lists only deletes (Melvin, 2026-11-22). The website's Channels page also adds, renames and deletes lists in the account backup. Deleting a list leaves its channels and films where they are. Channels show in the order they were added; films in playlist order. Shows aren't added to your own lists.
- **Favourites** are free on phone, Android TV and the website (Melvin, 2026-11-22: "favourite for all 3 phone, android and website"). The heart on Live TV and the website channel details toggle the same backup entry. The Samsung TV app does too: yellow on a channel, or Favourite on a movie's page, changes the profile's Favourites in the account's backup, and its lists, Add to list (blue on a channel) and Settings › Your lists (delete only) work as on Android TV, with renamed channels and logos shown but not changed ([Samsung TVs](tizen.md)). The Vega Fire TV app does the same: hold OK on a channel, or Add to list on a movie's page, opens Add to list with Favourites first, and Settings › Your lists deletes ([Vega OS](vega.md)).
- A list holds stable ids, like a favourite, so a refresh keeps it. Each profile has its own lists, deleting a profile deletes them, and the backup in the Tuned account carries the profile's lists along with its favourites ([sync](sync.md)). A list's id is chosen at random when it's made, so the same list has the same id on every device.
- **Premium** in the store build: making and using your own lists. A locked "Add to list" opens the Premium screen ([premium](premium.md)).

## Renaming a channel and changing its logo

Melvin, 2026-11-19: "do the rename if people want it. yes it should be premium." On phones, tablets and the website, a live channel can be **renamed** and have its **logo changed**. On phones and tablets the channel's page has those as buttons beside **Add to list** (Melvin, 2026-11-19: "Optimize TV for remotes, avoid 3 dot drop downs"), and its long-press menu has them too. **Android TV leaves both out** (Melvin, 2026-11-22: "Don't allow renaming channels on android tv", then "android tv dont change logo, website change logo"), so typing stays off the remote; Favourites and Add to list remain. The website's Channels and guide page edits the same backup the apps sync ([accounts](accounts.md)).

- A new name shows everywhere at once: the lists, search, the guide, the player and the widget, because it is written over the cached channel (`channel_edits`, Room v19, and `ChannelEditor`). Search finds the provider's name as well as the new one. "Use the original name" puts the provider's back.
- A new logo is one of the logos other copies of the channel use (the HD copy's logo for an FHD copy that has none), or a picture link. "Use the original logo" puts the provider's back.
- Each refresh lays the edits over the provider's copy before the cache takes it (`ChannelEdits.applyTo`), noting the provider's current name and logo as the originals, so a refresh never undoes an edit. Backup copies and the HQ button still match a renamed channel by the provider's name, so renaming "US| ESPN FHD" to "ESPN" doesn't lose its copies.
- Edits belong to the playlist, not a profile (a channel is called the same whoever watches), and sync carries them.
- Premium in the store build, like your own lists.
