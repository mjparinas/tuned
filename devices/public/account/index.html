What Tuned does

Everything below is in the app on your phone, tablet and TV.

Every kind of playlistXtream logins, MAC portals, and any M3U link or file, with its XMLTV guide.
Opens straight awayChannels and the guide are kept on your device and refreshed in the background, so nothing waits on the internet.
Live TV, Movies and ShowsSorted for you, with search, favourites and Continue Watching.
A proper TV guideThe grid guide, channel numbers, and flip back to the last channel.
A player that gets out of the wayJust the video until you tap. Captions, subtitle search, sound tracks and picture-in-picture.
Cast to your TVChromecast and DLNA TVs, saved films and episodes included.
Live eventsToday's games and fights from the guide. Tune finds a working English stream, and backup copies take over if a channel drops.
Real names and detailsPosters, ratings, cast and episode names, and a page for each actor showing what's in your playlist.
Made for Android TVWorks with the remote, and you can sign a TV in from your phone.
One connection at a timeTuned never opens two streams at once, and downloads wait while you watch.
Pause, rewind and record live TV PremiumGo back in time, jump to live, and save what you're watching.
Watch offline PremiumSave films and episodes to your device. More than three at a time with Premium.
Profiles PremiumUp to six, with kids profiles and PINs.
Your own lists PremiumLists beside Favourites, and rename channels or change their logos.
Follow events PremiumGet an alert before a game you follow starts.
Backup in your Tuned account PremiumEvery device you sign in keeps the same playlists, lists and settings.
Trakt and themes PremiumScrobble what you watch to Trakt, and pick your own colours.
