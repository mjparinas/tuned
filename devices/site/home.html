How it works

From login to live TV in a minute

  1. 1

    Add your playlist

    Type the login from your TV provider, paste a link, pick a file, enter a MAC portal, or connect Plex, Jellyfin or Emby. On a TV, scan a code and type it on your phone instead.

Questions

Good to know

Does Tuned come with channels?

No. Tuned is a player: it plays the playlist from your own TV provider or home media server, through an Xtream Codes login, an M3U link or file, a MAC portal, or Plex, Jellyfin or Emby. It doesn't sell or include any channels.

How do I install it?

On a phone or tablet, tap Download for Android, open the file, and allow installs from your browser when Android asks. On Android TV or Google TV, install the free Downloader app from the TV's app store, type tunedplayer.com/download into it, and allow it to install apps. The Android TV guide has every step, and the Fire TV guide covers Fire TV Sticks. After that Tuned offers each new version itself.

Is it free?

Yes. Tuned is free, with no ads. Tuned Premium is an optional upgrade for the extras marked Premium in the list above.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones and tablets on Android 8.0 or later, and Android TV and Google TV. Google Play and a Samsung TV app are coming soon.

Is my login safe?

Your login is encrypted on the device and stays there until you sign out. It never goes in the app's log. If you turn on a Tuned account, its backup carries your playlists to your other devices.

Will it get my account blocked for streaming twice?

Tuned only ever opens one stream connection. The old stream closes before the next opens, nothing loads ahead, and downloads wait while you watch.
