## Price

On Apple devices GSE Smart IPTV Pro is a US$2.99 purchase. On Android there is a free version with ads. Tuned is free with no ads; Premium is US$9.99 a year with a 7-day trial or US$24.99 once.

Right now Tuned downloads from this site, through GitHub Releases, and that version is free with every feature unlocked, Premium ones included. The Premium prices are for the Google Play version, which is coming soon; they are what Play charges in the United States, and Play shows them in your own currency. Premium on Play covers up to three devices, and a Tuned account can add more.

## Where GSE Smart IPTV is ahead

- **Apple devices.** GSE runs on iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac. Tuned is Android only.
- **AirPlay** on Apple devices.
- **A low one-time price** on iOS.

## Where Tuned is ahead

- **No ads on Android.**
- **Catch-up, pause and rewind live TV, and offline downloads.**
- **A full grid guide** with reminders, and a Live events page.
- **Film and show pages** from TMDB, Trakt and subtitle search.

## What Tuned does that most players don't

- **Opens straight to your channels.** The playlist and guide live on the device, so the app shows channels the moment it opens and keeps them fresh in the background. Lists page straight from the database, so a playlist with tens of thousands of streams scrolls smoothly.
- **One stream connection, ever.** The player, downloads, recordings, channel scans and backup copies take turns on a single connection, and anything in the background waits while you watch. Providers limit connections per account, and Tuned never uses a second one.
- **Live events from your guide.** Today's games and fights are pulled out of the guide into one page. Tune finds a working English stream for an event, one channel at a time, and you can follow a team, league or fighter to get an alert before they play.
- **Backup copies and dead channels.** When a channel has several copies, Tuned tries the next one if the first won't play, and a channel scan hides channels that never work, without blaming them for a slow internet connection or a busy server.
- **Real names over numbers.** Episodes show their real titles from TMDB rather than "S1 E1", and tapping an actor opens everything they've been in, with what's in your playlist marked.
- **Sign in on a TV with your phone.** Scan a QR code on the TV and type the login on your phone instead of with the remote.

## Which one to pick

If you're on Apple devices, GSE is one of the cheaper options. On Android and Android TV, Tuned does more, with no ads.
