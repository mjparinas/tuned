Tuned 2.4.0 is out: 1 new.

## New

- The category and filters you pick on Live TV, Movies and Shows stay picked until you change them, even after closing Tuned or restarting your device
- Each profile keeps its own filters, so switching to another profile shows the ones it chose, and a new profile starts with nothing picked
- A category that has gone since, such as one you hid, goes back to All, so a tab never opens empty
