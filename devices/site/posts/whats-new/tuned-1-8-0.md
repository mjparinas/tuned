Tuned 1.8 makes the player, guide and Settings work with TalkBack and large text, and adds search in Settings.

## New

- Cleaner episode names, undo a hidden group, search everything from an empty search
- Roomier home search, a More menu in the player, and search in Settings

## Fixed

- Catch-up kept on an unchanged playlist, event alerts tap their own event, Trakt hears where Next left off
- Make the player, guide and settings work with TalkBack and large text
- The player honours the parental lock and kids profiles from the tile, widget and TV cards
