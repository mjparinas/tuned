Tuned 1.3 is about speed and staying up: faster Live TV and Shows, backup copies when a channel dies, a full grid guide, pause and rewind live TV, and actor pages.

## New

- Switch to a backup copy when a channel dies, and hide channels that won't play
- Group the Downloads page by show and season
- Account status in Settings, and hold playback when every connection is in use
- Crash reports, developer mode with a log viewer, and crash guards everywhere
- Match the TV's frame rate to the video, and pick a playable audio track on silent channels
- Auto-tune finds "UFC 335" however it's written, tries US and Canada first, and has a Next stream button
- Offline banner and offline playback, with cache-first artwork and details
- A full grid TV guide on Live TV
- Tap an actor to see their films and shows, oldest first
- Downloads lists each show once, then its seasons and episodes, with space left
- Pause and rewind live TV
- Episode screenshots and names from TMDB on show pages

## Faster

- Faster Live TV and Shows tabs, skeletons, database upkeep

## Fixed

- Fast search, search during refresh, instant favourites
- Make the new-version prompt reliable, and test it against GitHub's real answer
- Play finished downloads from the device instead of failing
- Never hide sports, PPV and event channels, and let auto-tune reach empty event slots
- Read countries, languages, adult categories and sections the way other providers write them
