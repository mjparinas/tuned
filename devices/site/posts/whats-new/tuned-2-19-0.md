Tuned 2.19.0 is out: 1 new, 1 changed, 2 fixed.

## New

- Favourite a whole show: the heart on a show's page, or in its poster's menu, saves the show itself, and Shows › Favourites lists it

## Changed

- Watch later is now part of Favourites. The two did the same job, so the bookmark button is gone from film and show pages and poster menus, and the heart does it instead. Everything you had saved to Watch later moves into Favourites by itself the first time you open 2.19.0, on every profile, keeping when you added it. Nothing is lost, and the Watch later row on Movies and Shows is now a Favourites row, newest first

## Fixed

- Changing a playlist, a setting, a favourite or a list on the website no longer loses favourites, lists or renamed channels in the app
- The account sign-in page sits in the centre of the screen, and the Google and Apple buttons match
