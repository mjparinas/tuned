Tuned 2.1.0 is out: 16 new, 3 fixed.

## New

- Put Premium above Devices on the account page and confirm deleting the backup
- Clearer home page: app first on phones, badges once, TV install help, guides in From the blog
- Widen the account page on desktop
- Search the whole guide
- Films and shows for MAC portals
- Manage playlists and settings from the website, synced both ways
- Pause, rewind and record live HLS channels
- More Live TV filters (quality, on now, guide, catch-up, watched)
- Show covers on the Shows grid instead of episode stills
- Translate the app into 11 languages
- Play Dolby and DTS sound on devices that can't decode it
- Switch back to a recent playlist instantly
- Record programmes from the guide
- Sort Movies and Shows by newest, year, rating or A to Z
- Better search titles, social cards, breadcrumbs and links between posts on the website
- Lay out the web account like a desktop app, with a side menu and an overview

## Fixed

- Show every sample show's poster in the screenshots
- Show a show's wide picture whole over a blurred backdrop instead of cropping it
- Let Stripe Tax read a returning customer's address at checkout
