Tuned 2.17.0 is out: 2 new, 1 changed.

## New

- Getting to know Tuned: after you make a Tuned account, a short email each day shows one feature with a picture of it, from the guide and your own lists to the TV app and how Tuned stays fast. Premium features are marked Premium
- Your account has a Daily tips by email switch, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe link

## Changed

- Tuned's emails now share the app's dark look
