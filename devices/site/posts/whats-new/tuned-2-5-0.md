Tuned 2.5.0 is out: 3 new, 8 fixed.

## New

- Turn the phone into a remote while casting
- Show progress for installing updates and for every refresh, download and sync
- Make the website's channel page a full guide like the app

## Fixed

- Say why Install does nothing on a TV, and log every install step
- Make TV search boxes work with the remote
- Make Zoom and Stretch change the picture on a TV
- Build the first download's channel list in one go, much faster on TV boxes
- Fit the first download's steps on a TV screen
- Put the mic first on TV search and focus it when search opens
- No bottom sheets on a TV: What's new and Live TV's Filters fill the screen, remote-friendly
- Make every Settings screen work with a TV remote
