Tuned 1.0 adds search everywhere (with your voice), Continue Watching, guide reminders, player gestures and tools, and a parental PIN for adult categories.

## New

- Accent colour choices in Settings
- Continue Watching and recently watched channels
- Parental PIN for adult categories
- Player gestures and automatic reconnect
- Guide reminders
- Search everywhere, with voice
- Order favourites
- Player tools: mini guide, aspect and zoom, quality, stream info, screen lock

## Fixed

- Back button on the full-screen player
