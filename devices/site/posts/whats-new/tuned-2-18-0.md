Tuned 2.18.0 is out: 3 new, 1 changed, 1 fixed.

## New

- Tuned runs on Fire TV: the same TV app, with the rail, focus and remote keys you know from Android TV, on every Fire TV from Fire OS 7 on
- On a Fire TV, Premium comes with your Tuned account: sign in and an account with Premium unlocks it on that TV
- 27 new guides on the blog, each with real screenshots: setting up M3U, Xtream and MAC portal playlists, fixing a playlist that won't load, parental controls, Trakt and Simkl, offline downloads, the guide, casting and more

## Changed

- Every blog post and post card has a picture of its own

## Fixed

- An example in Settings now uses a made-up channel name
