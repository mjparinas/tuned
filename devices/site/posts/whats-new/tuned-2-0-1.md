Tuned 2.0.1 is out: 9 fixed.

## Fixed

- The home page's hero shows Opens straight to your channels instead of the version number
- Grey out subtitle timing while subtitles are off
- Films and shows filed under EN show with only Canada and the United States on
- Show the logo on a dark splash screen in light mode
- Fit every welcome option on a TV screen
- Settings on a TV no longer crashes while channels load
- Harden Google sign-in, Play checks, code redemption and the website's CSP
- Keep the app running after failures it didn't handle, and restart it instead of crashing
- The privacy policy gets its own page on the website at /privacy
