Tuned 2.21.0 is out: 2 changed, 1 fixed.

## Changed

- Live TV, Movies and Shows show their first rows at once, even with tens of thousands of channels. Tuned no longer counts the whole list before drawing it: the rest fills in as you scroll, and the count is kept for next time, so going back to a tab, a sort or a filter shows the full list straight away
- Each tab keeps its place: switch from Movies to Live TV and back, and you're where you left off, with the remote on the same poster on a TV

## Fixed

- Going back from a screen fades like every other screen change, instead of shrinking away
