Tuned 1.5 adds the Google Play build with Tuned Premium, the HQ button, Jump to live, a scroll bar on long lists and a time zone setting for the guide.

## New

- A Google Play version, coming soon
- Copy button beside the Trakt and Google sign-in codes
- Tuned Premium with Play Billing in the store build, locks on Premium features, and one Premium screen
- Hide 24/7 in Movies and Shows too
- Scroll bar with drag-to-jump on Live TV, Movies and Shows
- Show downloaded shows and films as posters
- Jump to live button on paused live TV
- HQ button switches between a channel's 4K, FHD, HD and SD copies, hidden when there are none
- Time zone setting for guide and event times
- Sync between devices without a passphrase

## Fixed

- Sort countries alphabetically in Settings
- Skip saving an unchanged playlist and keep scheduled refreshes out of sight
- Show Google's real reason when Drive sync sign-in fails
- A tap hides the player controls without them popping back up
- Cast saved MKV episodes as MP4 with AAC sound, send each load once, and keep 0% spots from Trakt
- Keep other countries out when only some are chosen
