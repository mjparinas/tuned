Tuned 1.12 adds signing in on a TV with your phone, the new channel stack icon, a cleaner player with fit and lock buttons, and remote-friendly screens on TVs and tablets.

## New

- Player clock, stream info pills and accent seek bar
- Sign in on a TV with your phone
- Settings on the side rail; no menu on TV
- Replace the player's three-dot menu with fit and lock buttons
- Switch the app icon to the channel stack
- No hidden menus: every long-press action is a button, Settings in the phone bar
- TV remote focus, Live TV for the big screen, and Settings side by side on tablets

## Fixed

- Stop Skip intro following you past the intro
- The TV welcome screen fits four cards without scrolling
- Make the profile button as tall as the search box
- Colour the profile names on Who's watching themselves, so they read on the dark theme
- The bottom bar stays on Downloads
- Show each show's main poster on Downloads and the Shows tab
- Keep dialogs off the screen's edges and fit every accent swatch
