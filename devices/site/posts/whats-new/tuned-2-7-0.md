Tuned 2.7.0 is out: 5 new, 8 fixed.

## New

- Manage lists in Settings; drop rename/delete chips on browse
- Cinematic full-screen TV show page with season tabs
- Website channel edits and lists; hide logo change on TV
- Calibrate TV overscan with tighter default margins
- Dedicated TV layouts for Movies and Shows

## Fixed

- Hide channel rename on Android TV
- Remove downloads from Android TV
- Keep TV channel chips horizontal
- TV search uses Tuned keyboard and inline voice
- Soften Who's watching focus ring on TV
- Streamline Android TV for remote-first watching
- Drop unsupported TV GMD and add missing plurals
- Format ParentalPin for spotless and detekt
