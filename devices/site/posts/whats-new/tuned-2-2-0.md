Tuned 2.2.0 is out: 3 new, 6 fixed.

## New

- See real progress and numbered steps while your channels load
- Start watching Live TV from your Tuned account when your provider doesn't answer, then get the rest from the provider later
- Keep your channel list in your Tuned account, so a new or reset device can start from it

## Fixed

- "Getting your channels ready" can no longer spin forever: it says what went wrong and offers Retry
- Retry on that screen now always starts over
- Keep sign-in codes on one line and widen the TV code dialog
- Fill the whole TV screen behind Who's watching
- Keep the favourites widget's tiles their size, showing more channels when it's made bigger
- Stop setting choices from being cut off
