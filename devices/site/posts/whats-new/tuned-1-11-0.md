Tuned 1.11 is a big one: your own lists beside Favourites, MAC portals, renaming channels, sound delay, background play, lighter mode for slow TV boxes, a new look for Settings, and What's new.

## New

- News, Kids, Movie channels, Music, Documentaries and Adult filters in Live TV
- Playback speed for films and episodes, never on live TV
- Sound delay setting for lip sync
- Tuned logo on Who's watching, and at most six profiles
- Tunnelled playback on Android TV
- Keep playing in the background, with a setting to turn it off
- Say to check the date and time when the device's clock is wrong
- Your own lists beside Favourites, like a music app's playlists
- Check every theme colour reads at 4.5:1, and deepen Blue's light tone
- Settings in headed cards with coloured badges, and What's new
- Limit Premium to three devices per purchase
- Save a report when the app freezes
- Pin groups to the front, and shortcuts on the app icon
- Rename channels and change their logos
- Report a problem in one tap
- Screenshots of every screen drawn on the JVM, on demand
- Show casting progress with a spinner
- Control the TV from the player while casting, with subtitles
- User agent picklist with a custom option
- Restore from Google Drive on the welcome screen, and sync profiles and every setting
- Lighter mode for weak TV boxes
- Sign in to a MAC (Stalker) portal
- Sign out resets the app to how it was installed

## Fixed

- Readable text on Who's watching in the dark theme
- TV sign-in fixes: screens inside a TV's edges, visible password, no default server, update offer on welcome
- A cast stopped on the TV no longer takes the next show
