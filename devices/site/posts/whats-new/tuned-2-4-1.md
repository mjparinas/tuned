Tuned 2.4.1 is out: 2 fixed.

## Fixed

- Channels, films and shows load again on TVs and phones running Android 12 or older, such as the NVIDIA Shield, instead of stopping at "Couldn't load your channels"
- Top rated on Movies and Shows sorts by the star rating each poster shows, filling in ratings in the background with progress above the grid, instead of staying in playlist order when the provider sends no rating
