Tuned 2.0 is a new install with a new app id, ready for Google Play. Your Tuned account brings your playlists, settings and profiles across in one tap.

## Moving from 1.13

Tuned 2.0 installs beside your old copy instead of replacing it, because the app's id changed. To move across:

1. In the old copy, check you're signed in to your Tuned account, so your latest backup is saved.
2. Install Tuned 2.0 and choose "Restore from your Tuned account" on the welcome screen.
3. Once everything is there, uninstall the old copy. Downloads stay with the old copy, so download them again in 2.0.

## New

- Report a problem asks what happened, then sends it straight to us with Send to Tuned, or lets you share it yourself
- Anonymous usage stats help us see what to fix first. Switch them off any time in Settings › Updates and advanced › Privacy
- Tuned's website is now www.tunedplayer.com, with guides, how-tos and comparisons to help you set up your playlist

## Fixed

- Sign-in, backups and signing in on a TV with your phone now use www.tunedplayer.com
