Tuned 1.4 adds profiles with kids profiles and PINs, the Live Events page, recording, channel numbers, guide fixes, Trakt and subtitle search.

## New

- Live Events page with Tune, following and alerts
- Hide 24/7 channels, honour per-stream headers, and Xtream's adult flag
- TMDB episode names in Continue Watching, the player and Downloads
- Read the Xtream API when a provider blocks the M3U download or leaves shows out
- Move a channel's guide times by its tvg-shift
- Profiles with their own favourites and history, kids profiles and profile PIN locks
- Guide fixes: time offsets, extra guide links, and picking a channel's guide entry
- Channel numbers, typing a number on the remote, and flip back
- Welcome screen and Settings › Playlists for any number of Xtream, M3U link and M3U file playlists
- Open the stream in another player
- Start on the last channel at launch
- Android TV home screen rows
- Settings split into pages by area
- Find subtitles on OpenSubtitles for films and episodes that have none
- Build the TMDB key in from a release secret instead of Settings
- Record the live channel being watched
- Trakt sign-in with a code, and scrobbling films and episodes
- Sync playlists, settings and favourites between phone and TV through Google Drive

## Fixed

- Casting to a TV works for saved episodes, closes the phone's stream first, and logs every step
- Sign in to Trakt without a client secret
