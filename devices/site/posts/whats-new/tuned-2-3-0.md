Tuned 2.3.0 is out: 2 new, 1 fixed.

## New

- Who's watching shows up at once when you open Tuned, with background work waiting until the first screen is up
- On a TV, the remote starts on the first profile in Who's watching, so one press of OK picks it

## Fixed

- A sort you pick on Movies or Shows stays picked, even while your account's backup runs
