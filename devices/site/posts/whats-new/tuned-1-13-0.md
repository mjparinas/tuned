Tuned 1.13 adds Tuned accounts, so every device keeps the same playlists and settings, plus new home screen widgets and casting that ends when you close the player.

## New

- Closing the player ends the cast
- Dots on the app icon only for reminders, alerts and new versions
- Favourites widget with channel logos, and a Continue watching widget
- Tuned accounts: sign in with Google, and a backup keeps every device the same, in place of Google Drive sync
- Devices per Tuned account, extra devices from Google Play, and Send to a TV signs the TV in to your account
