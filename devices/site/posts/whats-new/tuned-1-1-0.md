Tuned 1.1 brings film pages with ratings and trailers, downloads for watching offline, casting to Chromecast and DLNA TVs, catch-up, and Android TV.

## New

- Encrypted backup and restore
- Chromecast from the player
- Favourites widget and last-channel quick-settings tile
- Cast to DLNA TVs from the same picker
- Catch-up on channels that keep recordings
- Ratings, summaries and trailers from TMDB
- Switch between saved playlists
- Film details page, poster ratings and an on-device cache
- Download films and episodes to watch offline
- Android TV launcher, focus outlines and remote keys in the player
- Downloads page in the bottom bar and download a whole season

## Fixed

- Only a BREAKING CHANGE footer bumps the major version
- Details page, offline downloads, Android TV
