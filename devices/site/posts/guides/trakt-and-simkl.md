If you use Trakt or Simkl to keep track of films and shows, your IPTV playlist has probably been a blind spot. Whatever you watch there doesn't count, so "up next" falls out of date and your history has gaps. Tuned fixes that: connect your account once and every film and episode you watch from your playlist is sent as you watch it.

Tuned is a player for your own playlist and includes no content. Trakt and Simkl only receive what you watch, never your playlist or your login.

## What scrobbling means

"Scrobbling" is the app telling Trakt or Simkl what you're watching as you watch it: when you start, when you pause and when you stop, with how far through you are. Past 80%, the film or episode is marked watched. Pause halfway through a film and it shows up as in progress.

Tuned sends films and episodes. Live TV, catch-up and recordings aren't sent, since they don't match a film or show reliably. While you cast to a TV, the time comes from the TV, so it's still right.

## Connect Trakt or Simkl

1. Go to **Settings › Playlists and account** and tap **Connect Trakt** or **Connect Simkl**.
2. Tuned shows a short code and an address: trakt.tv/activate or simkl.com/pin.
3. Open that address on your phone or computer, sign in to Trakt or Simkl, and type the code. On a phone, the button beside the code copies it.
4. Tuned picks it up on its own within a few seconds.

You never type your Trakt or Simkl password into Tuned, which makes this ideal on a TV: the code goes on your phone, not the remote.

You can connect both. Each gets its own copy of what you watch. With a [Tuned account](/blog/guides/premium/), connecting once covers every device you sign in on.

## Bring your history into Tuned

Already watched half a season in another app? **Import watched history**, under Trakt or Simkl in Settings, reads what you've watched there and marks the same films and episodes watched in Tuned, for the profile you're using. A show's page then shows the episodes you've already seen, so you know where you are from day one.

The import shows each step as it goes, with a count and the time left, and says how many films and episodes it marked when it's done.

Nothing is sent back during an import, and items you finished more recently in Tuned are left as they are.

## How Tuned matches your playlist

Providers name things their own way: "EN | The Night Desk (2024) 4K", say. Tuned cleans off the tags and matches by title and year for films, and by show, season and episode for series, the same way it finds ratings and real episode names. No extra key or setup.

## Turn it off without disconnecting

**Scrobble what you watch**, in the same place, stops sending without signing out, if you're watching something you'd rather keep off your history. Disconnecting in Settings tells Trakt or Simkl to drop Tuned's access.
