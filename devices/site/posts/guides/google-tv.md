Google TV is Android TV with Google's home screen on top, so Tuned works the same way on both: the same app, the same remote controls, and the same TV layout. If you haven't set it up yet, start with [Tuned on Android TV](/blog/guides/android-tv/).

## Set it up in two minutes

1. Install Tuned (use the download button on this page).
2. Open it and choose **Sign in with your phone**.
3. Scan the QR code with your phone and fill in your playlist there, or send everything from Tuned on your phone. See [sign in on a TV with your phone](/blog/guides/sign-in-on-tv-with-phone/).

If you have a [Tuned account](/blog/guides/premium/), the TV can sign in to it the same way and restore your playlists, profiles and settings from the backup.

## Google TV's home screen

- Films and episodes you've started show in Google TV's **Continue watching** row.
- Your favourite channels get a **Tuned** row.

They update a few seconds after Tuned opens, after you watch something, and when you change a favourite.

## Voice and the remote

Tuned's player answers the system's media controls, so the remote's play and pause key, Bluetooth headphones and "Hey Google, pause" all work. You can turn this off with **Settings › Playback › Media buttons** if something keeps starting playback by itself.

## Smooth playback

Google TV devices vary a lot. If playback isn't smooth:

- keep **Match frame rate** on (the default),
- try **Tunnelled playback** in Settings › Playback (TV only),
- try another **Buffering** mode in Settings › Playback.

More in [fixing buffering](/blog/guides/buffering/).

## Casting to a Google TV

A Chromecast with Google TV is also a cast target, so you can send a channel or film from Tuned on your phone instead. See [casting](/blog/guides/casting/).
