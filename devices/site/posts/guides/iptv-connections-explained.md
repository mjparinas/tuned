"How many devices can I use?" is one of the first questions people ask about IPTV, and the answer surprises most of them: **your provider usually doesn't count devices at all. It counts streams playing at the same time.** Those are called connections.

## What a connection is

A connection is one stream coming from your provider's server: one channel, one film or one episode playing. A plan with **one connection** lets one thing play at a time. A plan with **two connections** lets two play at once, on two TVs, or a TV and a phone.

So you can install your player on as many devices as you like, and sign in on all of them. Only the number playing at the same moment matters.

## What happens when you go over

When every connection is in use and another device starts a stream, the server usually refuses it: a channel that won't start, or one that cuts out after a few seconds. Some providers go further and suspend an account that keeps going over, which is why it's worth staying inside the limit.

With an Xtream Codes login, Tuned shows **Connections in use** under **Settings › Playlists and account**, like "1 of 2". If every connection is already in use, probably on another device, the player tells you before it starts rather than failing silently, and you choose whether to go ahead.

## What secretly uses a connection

Some players use more connections than you'd expect:

- **Live previews:** a small moving picture of the channel you've highlighted is a second stream.
- **Several channels on screen at once** (multi-view): one connection per channel.
- **Downloads and recordings** running while you watch.
- **Channel checks** that test streams in the background.

Any of those can push a one-connection plan over without you doing anything wrong.

## Tuned uses one connection, always

Tuned was built so it never uses a second one. The player, downloads, recordings, channel scans and backup copies take turns on a single connection:

- **No live previews and no multi-view.** The channel list shows what's on in words and pictures from the guide, not a second stream.
- **Watching comes first.** While anything plays, downloads and scans stop and wait, and carry on when you stop.
- **Changing channel** closes the old stream before the new one opens.
- **Pause and rewind** keeps a copy of the stream you're already receiving, so going back never asks the provider again.

### Casting doesn't double up either

When you cast to a TV, the phone stops its own stream first, so the TV's stream is the only one. If the TV is turned away because the server still sees the phone, Tuned tries again once after 3 seconds.

### Downloading the playlist isn't a stream

Fetching the channel list and the guide are ordinary requests, not a stream, so setting Tuned up on a new device while someone else watches doesn't use their connection.

## Watching in more than one room

- **One connection:** one thing at a time. Tuned on the phone, the TV and the tablet is fine, as long as only one plays.
- **Two or more connections:** each room can watch its own channel. Ask your provider how many your plan has before adding a TV.
- **Setting up the other devices** takes seconds: on a phone, **Settings › Playlists and account › Playlists › Send to a TV** sends every playlist, your settings and favourites to a TV.
