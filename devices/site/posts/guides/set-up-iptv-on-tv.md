New to IPTV, or setting up a new TV? This guide goes from nothing to watching, in order. It takes about ten minutes, and you won't have to type a password with the remote.

## What IPTV is, in one minute

IPTV is live TV, films and shows sent over the internet instead of a cable or a dish. Two separate things make it work:

- **A provider** gives you the playlist: the list of channels and the login. That's who you pay for TV.
- **A player** is the app that plays the playlist and shows the TV guide. Tuned is a player.

Tuned includes no channels or content. It plays the playlist from your own provider, the same way a music player plays your own songs.

## What you need

1. **Your playlist details** from your provider, in one of these forms:
   - an **Xtream Codes login**: a server address, a username and a password;
   - an **M3U link** (ending in `.m3u` or `.m3u8`, or a `get.php` link), sometimes with a separate guide link;
   - a **MAC portal**: a portal address, registered to a MAC address.
2. **A TV that runs Android TV or Google TV**, or a box that does (a Google TV Streamer, an Nvidia Shield, an onn 4K box, a Chromecast with Google TV). Many Sony, TCL and Hisense TVs run Google TV already. No box yet? See [the best streaming box for IPTV](/blog/roundups/best-streaming-box-for-iptv/). On a Fire TV, see [Tuned on Fire TV](/blog/guides/fire-tv/).
3. **A steady connection.** A cable to the router is best; good Wi-Fi works too. Most HD channels need about 10 Mbps, and 4K about 25.
4. **Your phone**, to do the typing.

## Step 1: Install Tuned on the TV

Most TVs have no web browser, so you install Tuned with the free **Downloader** app:

1. On the TV, open the Google Play Store, find **Downloader** and install it.
2. Open Downloader and type **tunedplayer.com/download** into its address box.
3. When Android asks, let Downloader install apps, then choose **Install**.

Tuned shows up on the TV's home screen with its own banner, and keeps itself up to date from then on.

## Step 2: Add your playlist with your phone

Typing a server address with arrow keys is where most people give up. Tuned's first card on a TV is **Sign in with your phone**:

1. Press OK on **Sign in with your phone**. The TV shows a QR code and a short code.
2. Scan the code with your phone's camera. A page opens on the phone.
3. Fill in your Xtream login or paste your playlist link there. Pasting a whole `get.php` link fills in the login for you.
4. Check the four digits on the phone match the TV, and send. The TV asks before it saves anything.

Already using Tuned on your phone? Open **Settings › Playlists and account › Playlists › Send to a TV** on the phone and type the TV's code. It sends every playlist, your favourites and your settings at once.

Only your TV can read what the phone sends: the phone locks the login with a key that only the TV holds. More in [sign in on a TV with your phone](/blog/guides/sign-in-on-tv-with-phone/).

Prefer the remote? **Type it on the TV instead** takes you to the usual forms. See [adding an Xtream Codes playlist](/blog/guides/add-xtream-codes-playlist/) or [an M3U link](/blog/guides/add-m3u-playlist/).

## Step 3: Let the first download finish

Tuned downloads the channel list and the guide once and keeps them on the TV. The screen says what it's doing, in numbered steps with real progress. Big playlists take a minute or two the first time. After that, Live TV opens instantly and refreshes in the background.

## Step 4: Get to know Live TV

On a TV, Live TV's groups run across the top: Live events, Filters, Favourites, Recent and your provider's groups. Beside the list, the channel you're on shows what's on now and next, in words and pictures from the guide. There's no live preview, so there's still only one stream.

No guide showing? Xtream logins and MAC portals bring their guide automatically. An M3U playlist needs a guide link; see [setting up the TV guide](/blog/guides/tv-guide-epg/) and [the TV guide is empty](/blog/guides/blank-tv-guide/).

## Step 5: The remote, like a cable box

- **Up and down** change channel while you watch.
- **Number keys** jump straight to a channel.
- **The last-channel button** flips back to the one before.
- **The guide or menu button** opens the mini guide over the picture.

More tips in [Tuned on Android TV](/blog/guides/android-tv/).

## Step 6: Tidy it up

Big playlists carry thousands of channels from countries you'll never watch. In **Settings › Channels and lists**, hide the countries and groups you don't want, and tap the heart on the channels you do. Turn on **Start on the last channel** and Tuned opens straight into it, like a cable box.

## If something isn't right

- **Buffering?** Try a cable instead of Wi-Fi, and another buffering mode in Settings › Playback. See [fixing buffering](/blog/guides/buffering/).
- **An error when adding the playlist?** It usually means a typo, an expired account or a server that's down. [Provider errors](/blog/guides/provider-errors/) explains each one.
- **Picture cut off at the edges?** Settings › Appearance › **Calibrate screen** fits Tuned inside your TV's edges.

That's it: your playlist, on your TV, ready every time you turn it on.
