Tuned isn't in Amazon's Appstore, but most Fire TV devices run Fire OS, which is built on Android, so you can install Tuned yourself in about five minutes with the free Downloader app. This guide shows how, and how to tell whether your Fire TV can install apps at all.

As always: Tuned is a player for your own playlist. It includes no channels or content.

## First, check your Fire TV can install apps

Amazon has started moving some new Fire TVs to **Vega OS**, a system that isn't Android and can only run apps from Amazon's own store. On those, neither Downloader nor Tuned can be installed. As of November 2026, these run Vega OS:

- Fire TV Stick 4K Select (2025)
- Fire TV Stick HD (2nd generation, 2026)
- Fire TV Stick 4K (3rd generation, 2026)

Everything else, including the **Fire TV Stick 4K Max**, **Fire TV Stick 4K Plus**, **Fire TV Stick Lite** and **Fire TV Cube**, runs Fire OS and works.

To check yours, go to **Settings › My Fire TV › About**. If it says **Fire OS** (7 or 8 on a recent device), you're set. If it just says **OS** with a version starting with 1, it's Vega OS. Tuned needs Fire OS 7 or later (Android 9 underneath); Fire OS 6 and older are too old.

Buying a new device for IPTV? A Google TV box installs Tuned more simply, and has Ethernet built in. See [the best streaming box for IPTV](/blog/roundups/best-streaming-box-for-iptv/).

## Step 1: Turn on developer options

1. Go to **Settings › My Fire TV › About**. (On a Fire TV smart TV: **Settings › Device & Software › About**.)
2. Select the first line, your device's name, **seven times**, until it says you're already a developer.
3. Press **Back**. **Developer options** now shows under My Fire TV.

## Step 2: Install Downloader

1. On the Fire TV home screen, search for **Downloader** (the orange app) and install it. It's free.
2. Go to **Settings › My Fire TV › Developer options › Install unknown apps**, and turn **Downloader** on.

Downloader missing from that list? Some Fire OS 7 and 8 updates in 2026 had a bug that hid it. Restart the Fire TV and check again, or update the Fire TV (Settings › My Fire TV › About › Check for updates); Amazon's fix rolled out from September 2026.

## Step 3: Install Tuned

1. Open **Downloader**.
2. In its address box, type **tunedplayer.com/download** and choose **Go**.
3. When the download finishes, choose **Install**, then **Open**.
4. Back in Downloader, choose **Delete** to remove the installer file and free up space. Tuned stays installed.

Tuned keeps itself up to date from then on.

## Step 4: Sign in with your phone

Typing a server address with a Fire TV remote is slow. On Tuned's first screen, choose **Sign in with your phone**, scan the QR code with your phone's camera, and fill in your playlist there. Already use Tuned on your phone? Send everything with **Settings › Playlists and account › Playlists › Send to a TV**.

## Find Tuned on the home screen

Apps you install yourself land at the end of **Your Apps & Games**. Hold the remote's home button, choose **Apps**, find Tuned, press the menu button (three lines) and choose **Move** to put it in the front row.

## Using Tuned on Fire TV

Tuned works on a Fire TV the same as on Android TV: the same TV layout, the grid guide, and the remote shortcuts. Up and down change channel while you watch, and the remote's back button leaves the player.

A few Fire TV tips:

- **A Fire TV Stick has little memory.** Turn on **Settings › Appearance › Lighter mode**, or leave it on Automatic, and hide the countries and groups you never watch in **Settings › Channels and lists**.
- **Wi-Fi is the usual cause of buffering** on a stick. Amazon sells an Ethernet adapter that plugs into the stick's power. See [fixing buffering](/blog/guides/buffering/).
- **Picture cut off at the edges?** **Settings › Appearance › Calibrate screen** fits Tuned inside your TV.

## What's different from Android TV

Fire TVs have no Google services, so a few things that rely on Google are missing: Tuned's row on the home screen and Continue watching there, and casting to the Fire TV from a phone. Everything inside Tuned, the player, the guide, catch-up and downloads, works the same.
