A long flight, a train with patchy signal, a cabin with no internet: that's when a streaming playlist goes quiet. Tuned can save films and episodes from your provider's library to your phone or tablet before you leave, so they play anywhere, with no connection at all.

Tuned is a player for your own playlist and includes no content. It saves what your own provider offers, to your own device.

## Before you go: download

**A film:** open it and tap **Download**.

**A show:** open it and tap the download button beside an episode, or **Download season** to queue the whole season in order.

## Tips for a trip

- **Start the night before, on Wi-Fi.** A film is often 1 to 4 GB. To keep big files off mobile data, turn on **Wi-Fi only** in **Settings › Guide and refresh**; downloads follow it too.
- **Don't watch while it downloads, if you're in a hurry.** A download uses your provider's connection, so while anything plays, downloads pause and wait. They carry on, from where they stopped, the moment you stop.
- **Check a minute of each one** before you leave, especially if your provider is new to you.
- **A tablet is the best seat-back screen.** Tuned on a tablet has the same Downloads, on a bigger screen.

## On the plane: watch

Open Tuned. With no connection, a strip at the top says you're offline and offers **Downloads**. Everything you saved plays from the device, never the stream: from its page, from Continue Watching, or when the next episode starts. The poster, details and real episode names were saved with it, so it looks the same as at home.

Shows appear once on the Downloads page, as a poster. Tap for the seasons, then the episodes, each with its picture and name.

## What can and can't be downloaded

- **Films and episodes** from your provider's library: yes.
- **Live TV:** a live channel has no end, so it can't be downloaded. With [Premium](/blog/guides/premium/) you can **record** what you're watching, or schedule a programme from the guide, and recordings play offline like a film. See [pause, rewind and record live TV](/blog/guides/pause-rewind-record-live-tv/).

## Back home

Downloads stay until you delete them. Signing out of Tuned asks whether to delete them too, and how much space they take. Pick **Keep** and they still play.
