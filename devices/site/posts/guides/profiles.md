Profiles let everyone in the house keep their own **favourites, Continue Watching, watched marks and new-episode markers**, while sharing the same playlist, settings and downloads.

## Add a profile

1. Go to **Settings › Profiles and parental controls › Profiles**.
2. Tap **Add profile**, give it a name, and pick a picture.

You can have up to six. With more than one, Tuned opens on **Who's watching?** (turn that off with **Ask who's watching at start**), and your profile's picture sits beside the search box to switch.

## Make a kids profile

Turn on **Kids profile** when adding or editing a profile. A kids profile:

- shows only kids and family groups and channels,
- never shows adult categories, even with the PIN,
- has no Settings, so profiles can't be changed from it.

Leaving a kids profile asks for the parental PIN, so the first kids profile asks you to set one.

## Lock a profile with a PIN

Any profile can have its own PIN of 4 to 8 digits. Switching to it, or editing it from another profile, asks for the PIN. Forgot it? **Forgot PIN** takes your playlist password (or link) instead.

## Adult categories

Adult categories are hidden until you unlock them with the parental PIN (**Settings › Profiles and parental controls**). The unlock ends 5 minutes after Tuned leaves the screen, or when you switch profile, so a phone handed over later shows nothing adult. Five wrong PINs in a row wait 30 seconds.

The same rules apply everywhere a channel can be opened: widgets, Android TV home screen cards and the quick-settings tile included.

## Profiles on every device

With a [Tuned account](/blog/guides/premium/), profiles, their pictures, PINs and everything they keep travel in the backup, so a new TV has the same profiles as your phone.

In the Google Play version, more than one profile and the twelve Premium pictures are part of [Premium](/blog/guides/premium/).
