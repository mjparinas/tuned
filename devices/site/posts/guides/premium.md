Tuned is free, with no ads, ever. Premium unlocks the power features. Here's exactly what's in each, and how devices work.

## Free, for everyone

- One playlist (Xtream Codes, M3U link or file, MAC portal, or a Plex, Jellyfin or Emby server) with its TV guide
- Live TV, Movies, Shows, search, favourites and Continue Watching
- The whole player: captions and subtitle search, sound tracks, picture-in-picture, background play and casting
- The grid guide, channel numbers and flip back
- Film and show details, ratings, cast and real episode names
- Backup copies, auto-tune and the Live Events page
- Android TV and Google TV, with sign-in from your phone
- Up to three downloads at a time

## Premium adds

- **More than one playlist**, switched with a tap
- **Your own lists** beside Favourites, and renaming channels and changing their logos
- **Profiles**, with kids profiles, PIN locks and the Premium profile pictures
- **Pause and rewind live TV**
- **Recording** the channel you're watching
- **More than three downloads** at a time
- **Following events** and their alerts
- **A Tuned account** that backs up your playlists, settings and profiles and keeps your devices the same, plus the website's Channels and guide and Watch pages
- **Trakt and Simkl** scrobbling (and importing Trakt's watched history into Tuned)
- **Themes and accent colours** other than the default

## What it costs

In the Google Play version, Premium is bought through Google Play:

- **US$9.99 a year**, with a 7-day free trial, or
- **US$24.99 once**, for life.

Google Play shows the price in your currency. Nothing nags you to upgrade: a locked feature shows a lock, and tapping it opens the one Premium screen.

## Three devices per purchase

Premium covers **three devices**, such as a phone, a TV and a tablet.

- A device that has a seat keeps it.
- A device you haven't used for **60 days** gives its seat back by itself, so a lost phone never holds one for good.
- Reinstalling Tuned on the same device reuses its seat.
- **Settings › About › Tuned Premium › Your devices** lists the devices holding seats, and **Deactivate** frees one, from any device that has a seat.

Tuned never locks you out because of an outage: only a clear "all seats are taken" does. Offline, or with the server down, a device keeps what it last heard.

## More devices

With a Tuned account, you can add seats up to **10 devices**:

- **US$4.99 per device, once**, with lifetime Premium, or
- **US$1.99 per device a year**, with yearly Premium.

One-time and yearly can't be mixed: lifetime Premium takes one-time seats, yearly Premium takes yearly seats.

## The Tuned account

An account is optional; watching never needs one. You sign in with Google, on the website or in the app (on a TV, with your phone). It:

- **backs up** your playlists, settings, profiles, favourites, lists and watch later, so a new phone or TV restores them in one step,
- **holds your seats**, wherever you bought Premium,
- lets you **remove a device** on the website, even if you've lost all of them.

Signing out of the account frees that device's seat; your backup stays. You can delete the backup, or the whole account, on the account page any time. More in [what Tuned stores](/blog/guides/privacy/).
