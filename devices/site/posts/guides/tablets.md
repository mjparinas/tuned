On a tablet, an unfolded foldable or a Chromebook, Tuned doesn't stretch the phone screen. It changes layout to use the room.

<img src="/blog/img/guides/live-tv-tablet.webp" alt="Tuned's Live TV on a tablet, with the navigation rail on the left and group chips across the top" width="640" height="400" loading="lazy">

## What changes

- **A navigation rail.** Live TV, Movies, Shows, Downloads and Settings run down the left edge instead of along the bottom, under your thumb when you hold the tablet and out of the way of the list.
- **Bigger posters**, and more of them, in Movies and Shows.
- **List and details side by side.** On a wide screen, tapping a film, show or channel's guide opens it in a pane on the right while the list stays where it was. Back closes the pane.
- **The guide beside the video.** In landscape, the player's channel guide docks beside the picture, which shrinks to make room, instead of covering it.
- **Settings side by side**: the pages on the left, the page you picked on the right.

It follows the window, so split screen, folding and rotating all switch to the right layout as you go. On a phone everything looks as it always did.

## Good things to turn on

- **Picture-in-picture** (Settings › Playback, on by default): leave Tuned and the video keeps playing in a small window.
- **Keep playing in the background** (on by default): the sound carries on with the screen off, with controls in the notification.
- **Gestures**: drag on the left for brightness and on the right for volume, double-tap to skip, swipe sideways to change channel.
- **Lock** in the player's top row ignores touches, so a child holding the tablet can't change anything.

## Kids and profiles

A tablet is often shared. Give each person a [profile](/blog/guides/profiles/), and make a kids profile that shows only kids and family channels.

## Watching offline

Tablets travel. [Download](/blog/guides/recordings-and-downloads/) films and whole seasons before you leave; they play without a connection.
