If your playlist has sports, you know the routine: forty channels called "Sports 1" to "Sports 40", event slots that only get a name an hour before, and a guide that's in another time zone. Tuned turns all of that into one list of what's on today, in your time, with a button that finds a stream that works.

Everything here works with your own playlist. Tuned includes no channels, events or sports rights: it reads the channel names and guide your provider already sends.

## Live events: today's games in one list

Open Live TV and tap **Live events**, the first chip in the row. Tuned goes through your guide for the next 36 hours and picks out the matches, fights and pay-per-views:

- **One row per event.** The same game on six channels is one row that says how many channels have it.
- **In your time.** Times follow your phone's time zone, or the one you pick in Settings › Guide and refresh, split into Later today and Tomorrow.
- **Even when the guide is empty.** Providers often rename event slots instead of filling the guide ("PPV 7: Main event 10PM ET"). Tuned reads the event and the time from the channel's name, and converts the time zone too.
- **Filler left out.** Highlights shows, countdowns and paid programming don't count as events.

Live events is part of the free app.

## Tune: one tap to a stream that works

Event channels come and go, and the first one you try is often dead or in another language. **Tune** does the trying for you: it works through the channels showing the event and plays the first English stream that comes up, **one connection at a time**, so it never goes over your provider's limit.

Tune shows when the event is live or starts within the hour. Before that, the row has a bell instead, so you can set a reminder.

## Follow your teams, get an alert

With [Premium](/blog/guides/premium/), type a team, league or fighter into **Follow** at the top of Live events. Events you follow come first, and after each guide refresh Tuned sets an alert before each one. Tap the alert and Tuned tunes the event for you.

<figure><img src="/email/follow.jpg" alt="Followed teams as chips, and an alert saying Harbour Hawks start in 15 minutes, tap to tune" width="1200" height="760" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Follow a team, and the alert takes you straight to the game.</figcaption></figure>

- Names must match a whole word, so following a short name won't catch unrelated teams.
- The alert comes as long before as **Remind me before a programme** in Settings › Guide and refresh (5 minutes by default).
- Unfollowing removes the alerts it set.
- On a TV, follow from your phone or the website; the TV shows the alerts and the list.

## Find any game with search

Search looks through the whole guide, not just channel names. Type a team and you'll see every channel showing it now and later, with **Auto-tune** at the bottom to play the first one that works.

## Missed the goal? Rewind it

With Premium, live TV works like a recording: pause any channel, rewind up to the last 30 minutes, and tap **Live** to jump back to now. You can also record the game you're watching. See [pause, rewind and record live TV](/blog/guides/pause-rewind-record-live-tv/).

## Put it on the big screen

Watching on your phone? Cast the game to a Chromecast, Google TV or smart TV. The phone stops its own stream first, so it's still one connection. See [casting](/blog/guides/casting/).

## Tips for game day

- **Use a cable on the TV box.** Live sport is the least forgiving thing to stream.
- **Keep Match frame rate on** (Settings › Playback), so 50 and 60 fps sport plays smoothly.
- **Leave Switch to a backup copy on** (it's on by default), so a channel that dies mid-game switches to another copy of the same channel by itself.
- **Pick Smooth buffering** if your provider's sport channels pause now and then.

Tuned is a player for your own playlist. It includes no channels, events or content, and shows only what your provider's guide and channel names say.
