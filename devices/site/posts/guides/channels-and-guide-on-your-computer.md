Sometimes you want the guide on a bigger screen than your phone: to plan the evening, to check what time the game starts, or to watch a channel on the laptop while the TV is busy. With a Tuned account, the same Live TV guide you see in the app is on **tunedplayer.com**, in any browser.

Tuned is a player for your own playlist and includes no channels. The page shows the channels and guide from your own playlist, and a click hands the channel to VLC, which plays it straight from your provider. No video ever passes through Tuned.

## Open it

1. In the Tuned app, sign in to your [Tuned account](/blog/guides/premium/) (Settings › Playlists and account).
2. On your computer, go to **tunedplayer.com**, choose **Account** and sign in with the same Google account.
3. Pick **Channels and guide** in the menu.

The page opens at once with your channels and the next day of the guide, from the copy your Tuned app keeps in your account. It says which device it came from and how long ago ("From your Tuned app on Living room TV, 2 hours ago"). Your app brings the copy up to date about once an hour.

## The grid guide, like the app

Channels run down the side with their logos and numbers; time runs across the top, with a line at now. **Earlier**, **Now** and **Later** move the guide two hours at a time, and the times are in your own time zone.

## Search and filters

Search looks through channel names **and** what's on, so a team's name finds the game. **Filters** narrow it the same way as in the app: by type (sports, pay-per-view and events, news, kids, documentaries), quality (4K, FHD, HD), what's on now, country, and whether a channel has a guide. They stay chosen for that playlist in that browser.

## Click to watch in VLC

Click a channel, or a programme on now or still to come, and:

- **on a Mac, an iPhone, an iPad or an Android phone**, it opens in VLC, and the link is copied too;
- **on Windows or Linux**, the link is copied, ready to paste into VLC or any player you like.

A line at the bottom says what happened each time. VLC connects to your provider itself, so it's still your one connection, and you don't need Tuned's player open.

The whole playlist gets **Open in VLC**, a playlist file for VLC and **Copy playlist link** too, and those work even for providers that block web pages.

The page works in a phone's browser as well, with the same guide and filters, and a tap that opens the channel in VLC.

## Manage your playlists from the browser too

**Playlists and settings**, beside Channels and guide in the account menu, lets you add, edit or remove playlists and change the app's settings from a computer. Your devices pick the changes up the next time they back up.

## Privacy

Only the live channel list and the next day of its guide are saved to your account, to draw the page. Adult channels are never shared to it, channel links (which carry your login) aren't shown on the page, and streams never touch our server. Deleting your backup or your account deletes the copy too. More in [what Tuned stores](/blog/guides/privacy/).
