You spent an evening getting your IPTV just right: the login, the guide, hidden countries, favourites in order, a kids profile. Then the TV box dies, or you get a new phone, or you add a TV in the bedroom. With most players, you start again from nothing.

Tuned gives you two ways to bring everything across: **Send to a TV** from your phone, free, for setting up a TV in a minute; and a **Tuned account**, which backs everything up and restores it on any device.

Tuned is a player for your own playlist and includes no channels. What moves is your setup: the logins you added and how you arranged them.

## What comes across

| What                                      | Send to a TV | Tuned account |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------ | ------------- |
| Every playlist and its login              | Yes          | Yes           |
| Settings (guide, playback, hidden groups) | Yes          | Yes           |
| Favourites                                | Yes          | Yes           |
| Profiles, their pictures and PINs         | Yes          | Yes           |
| Your own lists                            | Yes          | Yes           |
| Trakt and Simkl connections               | Yes          | Yes           |
| Restore after the old device is gone      | No           | Yes           |

What never moves: downloads and recordings (they're files on one device, and stay there), a playlist added as a file from the device's storage, and a few settings that belong to one screen, like sound delay, lighter mode and screen calibration.

## Way 1: Send to a TV (free, a minute)

For setting up a TV from a phone that already has Tuned:

1. On the TV, install Tuned, open it and choose **Sign in with your phone**. It shows a short code and a QR code.
2. On your phone, go to **Settings › Playlists and account › Playlists › Send to a TV**.
3. Type the code from the TV, or scan the QR with the phone. Check the four digits match.
4. The TV lists what arrived and saves it only when you choose **Use it**.

Every playlist, your settings, profiles, favourites and lists arrive at once. (On a TV that's already set up, only the playlists are added, and it keeps its own settings. Without Premium, the TV keeps the playlist you're using on the phone.) Your phone locks them with a key only the TV holds, so nothing readable passes through our servers. If your phone is signed in to a Tuned account, the TV joins the same account too, without a second sign-in.

No Tuned on your phone yet? Scan the TV's QR code with the phone's camera instead: a page opens where you type your login once, and only the TV can read it. See [sign in on a TV with your phone](/blog/guides/sign-in-on-tv-with-phone/).

## Way 2: Your Tuned account (everything, anywhere)

A Tuned account is a backup of your whole setup, kept on our server and signed in with Google or Apple. Once your phone is signed in and backed up, any new device restores it in one step.

**Sign in on your current phone:** **Settings › Playlists and account › Sign in to your Tuned account**. Tuned backs up by itself from then on; **Back up now** does it straight away and says when it last did.

<figure><img src="/blog/img/guides/settings-page-playlists.webp" alt="Tuned's Playlists and account settings with Playlists, Sign in to your Tuned account and Sign out" width="270" height="586" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Settings › Playlists and account.</figcaption></figure>

**On a new phone or tablet:** install Tuned, and on the first screen pick **Restore from your Tuned account**. Sign in, and everything is back: playlists, profiles, favourites, lists, settings.

**On a new TV:** pick **Restore from your Tuned account** on the TV's first screen. It shows a QR code and a short code. Scan it, or go to tunedplayer.com/account on any phone or computer, sign in, and enter the code under **Pair a TV**. No typing with the remote.

### Keeping several devices the same

Every signed-in device backs up and joins the backup with its own changes, so a favourite added on the TV shows up on the phone. If two devices change the same thing, the newer change wins. The account keeps your last 5 backups. You can also manage playlists and settings from a browser at tunedplayer.com/account.

## Your Premium comes with you

Premium covers three devices. A new device takes a free seat; the old one gives its seat back after 60 days unused, so a lost or broken phone never holds one for good. To free a seat straight away, use **Settings › About › Tuned Premium › Your devices › Deactivate** on any device with a seat, or remove the device on the account page at tunedplayer.com, even if you no longer have it. See [Premium, seats and devices](/blog/guides/premium/).

## Moving from another player?

Different apps don't share favourites or settings, but your login works anywhere. See [switching from TiviMate or IPTV Smarters](/blog/guides/switch-from-tivimate-or-smarters/).

## Retiring the old device

Before you give away or recycle the old phone or box, open **Settings › Playlists and account › Sign out**. It resets Tuned to how it was installed, deleting every login on that device, and frees its Premium seat. Your account backup stays, ready for the next device.

The Tuned account is part of [Premium](/blog/guides/premium/): US$9.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or US$24.99 once. Send to a TV is free.
