Tuned is one app for phones, tablets and TVs. On an Android TV it shows its own banner on the home screen, fits inside the TV's safe area, and works with the remote alone. It runs on Android 8.0 and newer.

## Install it

Most TVs have no web browser, so use the free **Downloader** app:

1. On the TV, open the Google Play Store, search for **Downloader** and install it.
2. Open Downloader and type **tunedplayer.com/download** into its address box.
3. When Android asks, allow Downloader to install apps, then choose **Install**.

Tuned then keeps itself up to date.

## Sign in without typing

On the TV, Tuned's first card is **Sign in with your phone**. Scan the QR code and fill in your playlist on your phone, or send everything from Tuned on your phone. Step by step: [sign in on a TV with your phone](/blog/guides/sign-in-on-tv-with-phone/).

## Live TV made for the big screen

On a TV, the groups (Live events, Filters, Favourites, your lists, Recent and your provider's groups) run down a column on the left, so reaching one is a few presses. Beside the channels, the details of the one you're on: its logo, number, what's on now with the time and description, and what's next. These are words and pictures from the guide, never a live preview, so there's still only one stream.

The side rail has Live TV, Movies, Shows, Downloads, Search, Profile and Settings, and Live TV has a button beside search for the [grid guide](/blog/guides/tv-guide-epg/). There are no three-dot menus: every action is a button the remote can reach.

## The remote in the player

- **Up and down** change channel while the controls are hidden, like a cable box.
- **Channel up and down** (and page up and down) change channel any time.
- **Type a number** to jump to that channel.
- **The last-channel button** flips back to the channel you watched before.
- **The guide or menu button** opens the mini guide.
- **The info button** shows the controls, with the picture size, frame rate and sound format.
- In films, **left and right** skip back and forward while the controls are hidden.

Holding a channel button changes channel once, so you never flip through a string of channels and connections.

## In lists

- Page up and down, and channel up and down, jump a screenful through long lists.
- **Back** while scrolled down goes to the top first; the next Back leaves.
- Tuned remembers the last item you were on in each tab.

## Settings worth a look on a TV

- **Settings › Playback › Match frame rate** (on by default) switches the TV to the video's frame rate, so films at 24 fps and sport at 50 or 60 play smoothly.
- **Tunnelled playback** (TV only, off by default) hands the picture and sound to the TV's own hardware. Try it if 4K or 60 fps sport stutters.
- **Sound delay** moves sound up to 500 ms either way for a soundbar that lags.
- **Start on the last channel** (Settings › Channels and lists) opens straight into the channel you watched last, like a cable box.
- **Settings › Appearance › Lighter mode** helps cheap TV boxes with little memory. Automatic turns it on by itself when needed.

## On the TV's home screen

Films and episodes you're partway through show in the system's Continue watching row, and your favourite channels get a Tuned row of their own.
