Samsung TVs run Tizen and LG TVs run webOS, so Android apps like Tuned don't install on them. That doesn't mean you can't watch your playlist on one. There are two good ways, and neither needs anything installed on the TV itself.

Tuned is a player for your own playlist and includes no channels. You need the login or link from your provider, and Tuned on an Android phone or tablet.

## Way 1: Cast from your phone (nothing to buy)

Many Samsung and LG smart TVs can play video sent from a phone on the same Wi-Fi, using a standard called **DLNA** (or UPnP). TVs with **Chromecast built in** take a cast too. Tuned casts to both, and lists them together.

1. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi as the TV, and turn the TV on.
2. Open Tuned on the phone and play a channel, a film or an episode.
3. Tap the screen, then the **cast** button in the player's top row.
4. Pick your TV. Tuned shows "Connecting to…", then "Starting on…", then "Playing on…".

### Your phone becomes the remote

While casting, the phone stops its own stream, so your provider still sees **one connection**. Its controls drive the TV: pause, the seek bar, and next and previous to change channel or episode. You can lock the phone or switch apps and the TV keeps playing. Backing out of the player ends the cast.

### Your TV doesn't show up?

- Check the phone and TV are on the **same Wi-Fi** (not a guest network).
- Look in the TV's network or connection settings for an option that lets phones or other devices play to it, and turn it on.
- Restart the TV fully (unplug it for 30 seconds); some TVs stop answering after weeks on standby.

### Films you downloaded play too

A downloaded film or episode casts straight from the phone over your Wi-Fi, without touching your provider at all. More detail, including subtitles and formats, in [casting from Tuned](/blog/guides/casting/).

## Way 2: Add a streaming box to the TV's HDMI port

Casting is perfect for a film or a match. If the Samsung or LG is your main TV, a small Google TV box in its HDMI port is the better everyday setup: a remote, a grid guide on the big screen, channel up and down, and the app opening straight onto your channels.

<figure><img src="/blog/img/guides/browse-live-tv-tv.webp" alt="Tuned's Live TV on a TV, with group chips across the top, the channel list and what's on now" width="640" height="360" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Tuned on a Google TV box: Live TV with a remote.</figcaption></figure>

Good options include the Google TV Streamer, an onn 4K box or an Nvidia Shield. Plug it in, switch the TV to that HDMI input, and follow [how to set up IPTV on your TV](/blog/guides/set-up-iptv-on-tv/). Your phone does the typing, so no password with the remote. Which box to buy: [the best streaming box for IPTV](/blog/roundups/best-streaming-box-for-iptv/).

## Which way is right for you?

- **Watching now and then, or in a spare room:** cast from your phone. Free, and nothing to set up on the TV.
- **Your main TV, every day:** a Google TV box. It's quicker every time you sit down.

A Tuned app for Samsung TVs is on the way. Until then, both ways above give you your own playlist, with Tuned's guide and player, on a Samsung or LG screen.
