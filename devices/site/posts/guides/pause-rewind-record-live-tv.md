The doorbell goes during the final minute. With a cable box you'd pause. With most IPTV players, live TV just keeps going. Tuned's Premium makes live channels behave like a recording: pause, rewind, jump back to live, and record what you're watching or schedule a programme from the guide.

It all happens on your own device, with your own playlist. Tuned includes no channels, and there's no cloud DVR: nothing is stored anywhere but your phone or TV box.

## Pause and rewind live TV

Play any live channel, then use the player's usual controls:

- **Pause** stops the picture where it is. Press play and it carries on from there, a little behind live.
- **Rewind** with the back button or the seek bar, up to the last 30 minutes by default.
- **Fast-forward** to catch up, or tap **Live** (with the red dot, bottom right) to jump straight back to what's airing now.

### How long to keep

**Settings › Playback › Pause and rewind live TV** sets how far back you can go: 15, 30 (the default) or 60 minutes, or Off. Tuned keeps never more than 1 GB, and clears it when you change channel.

### Why it doesn't cost a second connection

Tuned keeps a copy of the stream it's already receiving, cut into small pieces on the device, as it plays. Rewinding reads those pieces; it doesn't ask your provider for anything. So your provider still sees one connection, and starting a channel is no slower.

It works with both kinds of live channel providers send: plain streams (`.ts`) and HLS (`.m3u8`).

## Record what you're watching

1. Play a live channel.
2. Tap **Record** in the player's top row. A small **REC** mark stays in the corner.
3. Tap Record again to stop. It also stops when you change channel, close the player, or the device has less than 500 MB free.

Recording saves the stream the player is already receiving, so it never opens a second connection. Pausing keeps recording.

## Schedule a recording from the guide

Open a channel's guide and tap **Record** beside any programme that hasn't ended, next to the reminder bell. Tuned records it in the background, from a little before it starts to a little after it ends (2 minutes each side by default; change it in **Settings › Playback › Start recordings early and end late**).

Watching always comes first. If you're watching something when a recording is due, it waits and records what's left once you stop. Two recordings can't overlap; Tuned tells you rather than cutting one short.

## Where recordings go

Recordings sit under **Recordings** on the Downloads page, newest first, with the programme's name from the guide, the channel, the time, the length and the size. They play from the device like a film, offline too.

On a TV box with spare storage (an Nvidia Shield, say), recordings have plenty of room. See [the best streaming box for IPTV](/blog/roundups/best-streaming-box-for-iptv/).

## What can't be recorded

Catch-up programmes (which you can already rewind), channels while casting, and a few HLS channels that can't be saved as one file (encrypted ones, for example). The Record button doesn't show for those.
