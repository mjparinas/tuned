"Free" means different things in IPTV players. Some are free with ads. Some are free for a week, then ask for a licence per device. A few are free with no ads at all. Here's how the popular players line up, from what each developer says on its own pages.

One thing first: a player is only the app. The channels come from your provider, and no player, Tuned included, comes with any. Be wary of any "free IPTV" that offers channels too; see [is IPTV legal?](/blog/guides/is-iptv-legal/)

It's US$9.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or US$24.99 once, for up to three devices.

## Why "free with ads" costs more than it looks

Ads in a video player aren't only annoying. They load from the internet while you watch, and on a busy TV box that can mean slower menus or a stutter in the stream. A player with no ads has nothing competing with your channel for the connection.

Tuned goes further: it only ever uses **one stream connection**. No live previews, no background downloads while you watch, so you stay inside your provider's limit. More in [how many devices can I use IPTV on?](/blog/guides/iptv-connections-explained/)
