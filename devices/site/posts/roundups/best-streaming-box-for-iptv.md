The box under your TV decides how fast channels change, whether sport looks smooth, and whether the app feels quick or sluggish. Here's what to look for, the boxes worth buying, and how to set each one up for IPTV.

A note first: these are ordinary streaming boxes, and Tuned is an ordinary player. Neither comes with channels. You bring the playlist from your own provider.

## What matters for IPTV

1. **Android TV or Google TV.** It runs the widest choice of IPTV players, Tuned included, and installs them from the Play Store or the free Downloader app.
2. **Ethernet.** Live TV can't buffer far ahead like a film can. A cable to the router fixes more stutter than any setting. A box with a port built in saves buying an adapter.
3. **Frame rate matching.** Films are 24 frames a second, sport 50 or 60. A box that switches the TV to match (Tuned does it for you) avoids the judder you see on pans.
4. **Enough memory.** Big playlists with tens of thousands of channels need room. 2 GB works; 3 GB or more feels quicker.
5. **Storage**, if you want downloads and recordings on the box.
6. **Sound passthrough.** Sports channels often carry Dolby Digital. A box that passes it to your soundbar keeps surround sound.

Prices change often, so check before you buy. Any recent Android TV or Google TV box will play your playlist in Tuned. These are simply the ones we'd pick.

### Nvidia Shield TV

Years after its launch, the Shield is still the box enthusiasts buy: quick, with Ethernet, plenty of storage for [downloads and recordings](/blog/guides/recordings-and-downloads/), and frame rate switching that just works. If money isn't the question, this is the answer. Settings in [Tuned on Nvidia Shield](/blog/guides/nvidia-shield/).

### Google TV Streamer

Google's own box, with Ethernet built in, and the Google TV home screen where Tuned's Continue watching and favourite channels appear. A good all-rounder. See [Tuned on Google TV](/blog/guides/google-tv/).

### onn 4K Pro

The value pick: a full Google TV box with Ethernet at a much lower price. Ideal for a second TV, or if you just want live TV to work.

### Fire TV

Fire TV runs Amazon's own software. Most models can still install Tuned with the Downloader app, but the newest ones run a system that can't install apps from outside Amazon's store at all, so check before you buy. Step by step: [Tuned on Fire TV](/blog/guides/fire-tv/).

## Day one: the settings that matter

Once Tuned is on the box (install it with Downloader and **tunedplayer.com/download**, then [sign in with your phone](/blog/guides/sign-in-on-tv-with-phone/)), go to **Settings › Playback**:

- **Match frame rate**: leave it on (the default).
- **Tunnelled playback** (TV only): try it if 4K or fast sport stutters.
- **Buffering**: Balanced suits most connections; **Smooth** if your provider's streams pause now and then.
- **Sound delay**: up to 500 ms either way, for a soundbar that lags.

Then fit the picture to your TV: **Settings › Appearance › Calibrate screen** moves and stretches Tuned until every corner mark sits inside the edge of your screen.

On a cheaper box with little memory, **Settings › Appearance › Lighter mode** helps the app stay quick. Automatic turns it on by itself when it's needed.
