TiviMate earned its place as the best-known IPTV player for Android TV, mostly for its grid guide. People look for an alternative for different reasons: they want a phone and tablet app that matches the TV one, a different price, a cleaner interface, or features TiviMate doesn't have. Here's what to check in a replacement, and how the options compare.

For a full feature-by-feature and price comparison, read [Tuned vs TiviMate](/blog/vs/tivimate/).

## What to keep when you switch

If you're used to TiviMate, make sure a replacement has:

- **A full grid guide** with catch-up, and a way to fix channels whose guide is wrong.
- **Several playlists**, Xtream Codes, M3U and, if you use one, MAC portals.
- **Favourites and your own groups**, and hiding the groups and countries you never watch.
- **Remote shortcuts**: channel up and down, number entry, last channel.
- **Recording** and **pause and rewind** of live TV.

## Tuned

Tuned is a free, ad-free player for Android phones, tablets and TVs, built around speed and getting out of your way.

- **It has what you'd miss:** a grid guide with catch-up and reminders, Xtream, M3U and MAC portals, favourites and your own lists, hidden groups and countries, channel numbers and flip back, recording and pause and rewind.
- **It adds:** sign-in on a TV with your phone, the same app on your phone with downloads for offline viewing, casting to Chromecast and DLNA TVs, subtitle search for films and episodes, real episode names and artwork, automatic backup copies for dead channels, auto-tune for live events, and profiles with kids profiles and PINs.
- **It stays on one connection.** Downloads, recordings and scans wait while you watch.
- **Moving over is quick.** If your TiviMate login is an Xtream Codes one, it's the same server, username and password in Tuned. See [adding an Xtream Codes playlist](/blog/guides/add-xtream-codes-playlist/), or [sign in on your TV with your phone](/blog/guides/sign-in-on-tv-with-phone/).

Free covers one playlist and most features; [Premium](/blog/guides/premium/) adds more playlists, profiles, recording, pause and rewind and a Tuned account backup.
