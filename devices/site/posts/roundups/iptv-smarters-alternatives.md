IPTV Smarters Pro is often the first player people meet, because many providers point new customers to it. It runs on a lot of devices, and an Xtream Codes login works in it straight away. People start looking elsewhere when they want a proper grid guide, a faster app, downloads for offline viewing, or simply something that feels cleaner on a TV.

The good news: your login isn't tied to Smarters. The same server, username and password work in any player that takes Xtream Codes, so trying another one costs you nothing.

## What to look for in a replacement

- **Xtream Codes and M3U**, so your current login works as it is. A MAC portal is a bonus if your provider uses one.
- **A full grid guide**, with catch-up for providers that record.
- **Speed**: a player that opens on your channels, not a loading screen.
- **A remote that works like a cable box**: channel up and down, number keys, last channel.
- **One connection at a time**, so you don't go over your plan. Players that show several channels at once use one connection per channel.
- **Clear pricing**, with no ads and no trial that locks you out.

## Tuned

Tuned is a free player for Android phones, tablets, Android TV and Google TV, built around speed and getting out of your way. It includes no channels: you add the playlist from your own provider.

<figure><img src="/blog/img/guides/browse-live-tv-tv.webp" alt="Tuned's Live TV on a TV, with group chips across the top, the channel list and what's on now" width="640" height="360" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Live TV on a TV: groups across the top, now and next beside the list.</figcaption></figure>

- **Same login, same channels.** Xtream Codes, M3U links and files, MAC portals, and Plex, Jellyfin or Emby servers.
- **A real grid guide**, with reminders, catch-up and a way to fix a channel whose guide is off.
- **Opens at once.** The playlist and guide are kept on the device and refreshed in the background.
- **Films and shows done properly**: posters, ratings and real episode names, downloads for offline viewing, and subtitle search.
- **No typing with the remote.** On a TV, scan a QR code and type your login on your phone.
- **Free with no ads.** [Premium](/blog/guides/premium/) (US$9.99 a year or US$24.99 once) adds more playlists, profiles, recording and pause and rewind on live TV.

## Other alternatives worth a look

Each link is a full comparison with Tuned, with what each developer says about price and ads.

- [TiviMate](/blog/vs/tivimate/): the best-known Android TV player, built around its grid guide. Made for TVs rather than phones.
- [OTT Navigator](/blog/vs/ott-navigator/): very configurable, for people who like to tweak everything.
- [Televizo](/blog/vs/televizo/): a popular free player for phones and TVs, with ads unless you buy Premium.
- [Sparkle TV](/blog/vs/sparkle-tv/): an Android TV player that also connects to home TV servers and tuners.
- [XCIPTV](/blog/vs/xciptv/): a common choice for Xtream Codes logins, and many providers ship branded copies of it.
- [Perfect Player](/blog/vs/perfect-player/) and [IPTV Pro](/blog/vs/iptv-pro/): lightweight, long-standing players.
- [VLC](/blog/vs/vlc/) and [Kodi](/blog/vs/kodi/): free media players that open an M3U link, without the IPTV extras.

Need one app on a Samsung or LG TV too? See [IBO Player](/blog/vs/ibo-player/), [Smart IPTV](/blog/vs/smart-iptv/) and [Duplecast](/blog/vs/duplecast/), or [watch your playlist on a Samsung or LG TV](/blog/guides/watch-iptv-on-samsung-lg-tv/) with Tuned on your phone.

## Moving your login from Smarters

In Smarters, your login is under "Login with Xtream Codes API": a server address, a username and a password. Type the same three into Tuned's **Xtream Codes login**.
