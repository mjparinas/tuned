There's no shortage of IPTV players for Android TV. The best one is the one that gets you to the channel fastest, stays smooth from the sofa, and doesn't fight the remote. Here's what to look for, how Tuned does it, and where to read how each popular player compares.

A note first: every player on this page, Tuned included, is just a player. None includes channels. You bring the playlist from your own provider.

## What matters on a TV

1. **Speed.** Opening the app should show channels at once, not a loading screen. Changing channel should take a second, not five.
2. **The remote.** Everything should be reachable with arrows and OK: no tiny menus, no long-press-only actions, and number keys that change channel.
3. **The guide.** A full grid guide, catch-up for providers that record, and a way to fix channels whose guide is wrong.
4. **Smooth playback.** Frame rate matching, a choice of buffering, and help when a channel dies.
5. **Set-up without typing.** Entering a server, username and password with a remote is painful.
6. **Staying inside your provider's limits.** Players that preview channels or download in the background can use a second connection.

## Why we built Tuned the way we did

- **Opens on the cache.** Your channels and guide are stored on the device, so Live TV shows instantly and refreshes in the background.
- **Made for the remote.** On a TV, groups run down the left, details of the focused channel sit beside the list, and every action is a button. Up and down change channel, number keys jump, and the last-channel button flips back.
- **A full grid guide**, catch-up for Xtream and M3U playlists, reminders, extra guide links and per-channel guide fixes.
- **Frame rate matching**, tunnelled playback for capable TVs, three buffering modes, and automatic switching to a backup copy of a channel that dies.
- **Sign in with your phone.** Scan a QR code and type the login on your phone, or send everything from Tuned on your phone.
- **One connection, always.** Downloads, recordings and channel scans wait while you watch, and nothing previews or prefetches.
- **Free, no ads.** Premium adds more playlists, profiles, pause and rewind, recording and a few more; see [what Premium gets you](/blog/guides/premium/).

## The popular alternatives

Each of these has a full side-by-side comparison with Tuned, including features and pricing.

**Made for Android TV and Android phones**

- [TiviMate](/blog/vs/tivimate/): the best-known Android TV player, known for its grid guide.
- [IPTV Smarters Pro](/blog/vs/iptv-smarters-pro/): a widely used player for Xtream Codes logins across many devices.
- [OTT Navigator](/blog/vs/ott-navigator/): a highly configurable Android player.
- [Televizo](/blog/vs/televizo/): an Android player with a TV guide and a premium upgrade.
- [Sparkle TV](/blog/vs/sparkle-tv/): an Android TV player with a TV-style guide.
- [Perfect Player](/blog/vs/perfect-player/): a long-standing, lightweight player.
- [XCIPTV](/blog/vs/xciptv/): an Xtream Codes player often supplied by providers.
- [IPTV Pro](/blog/vs/iptv-pro/): a long-running Android player.
- [iMPlayer](/blog/vs/implayer/): a player for Android TV and other platforms.
- [GSE Smart IPTV](/blog/vs/gse-smart-iptv/): a multi-platform player with broad format support.
- [OttPlayer](/blog/vs/ottplayer/): a multi-platform player.

**General media players that also play IPTV**

- [Kodi](/blog/vs/kodi/): the free, open-source media centre, with IPTV through an add-on.
- [VLC](/blog/vs/vlc/): the free all-round player, which opens M3U playlists.

**Mostly for smart TVs and other platforms**

- [Smart IPTV](/blog/vs/smart-iptv/), [SET IPTV](/blog/vs/set-iptv/), [Flix IPTV](/blog/vs/flix-iptv/), [IBO Player](/blog/vs/ibo-player/), [Duplecast](/blog/vs/duplecast/) and [Purple IPTV players](/blog/vs/purple-player/)
- [UHF](/blog/vs/uhf/), for Apple devices
