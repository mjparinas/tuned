# Tuned

A fast, quiet IPTV player for Android. Sign in to your TV service once, and Live TV, Movies and Shows are there instantly, with the guide, captions styled your way, audio language choice and picture-in-picture. Everything is cached on the device and refreshed in the background.

- **Live TV** with what's on now, a full schedule per channel, and next/previous to flip channels.
- **Movies and Shows** in poster grids, with seasons, episodes and resume.
- **Settings for nearly everything**: countries to hide, refresh schedules, buffering, captions (size, font, colours, edges, background, position), audio language, theme (light, dark, pure black, wallpaper colours) and more.

Built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose (Material 3), Media3 ExoPlayer, Room, Paging, WorkManager, DataStore, OkHttp and Coil. See [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for commands and conventions, and [docs/decisions](docs/decisions) for why things are the way they are.
